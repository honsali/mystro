# Mystro Reading Tasks

> Documentation axis: the six analytical tasks recognized by Mystro and the traditional apparatus
> that distinguishes each one. Technical architecture and report filenames are documented in their
> dedicated files.

Mystro recognizes six tasks. Each definition below owns its question, apparatus, historical line,
and implementation status.

## 1. Natal description

**Question:** Who is this person according to the natal promise?

Primary apparatus:

- sect and the diurnal/nocturnal hierarchy;
- whole-sign topical houses;
- planetary condition, dignity, debility, joys, and solar phase;
- Fortune, Spirit, hermetic lots, and family lots;
- prenatal syzygy;
- Mercury and Moon configurations;
- doryphory, dodecatemoria, fixed-star contacts, and triplicity phases;
- compatible Ptolemaic vitality and topical annexes.

Primary historical line: Valens, with explicitly labelled Ptolemaic and Dorothean-compatible
annexes.

**Status:** implemented as the production command-line JSON reading.

## 2. Life-arc prediction

**Question:** Which natal themes are activated during a period of life?

Primary apparatus:

- annual, monthly, and daily profections;
- Zodiacal Releasing;
- firdaria and decennials;
- distributions through bounds;
- zodiacal and mundane primary-direction variants;
- solar and lunar returns;
- lunations and eclipse evidence;
- bounded transits and planetary hours;
- evidence grouping across active chronocrators.

Primary historical line: Valens and Dorotheus, with medieval chronocrator, return, and direction
techniques kept explicitly labelled.

**Status:** implemented as gated local research reports.

## 3. Horary and event judgment

**Question:** What is the condition and likely perfection of one specific matter?

Primary apparatus:

- a chart for the question or event;
- quadrant houses selected by the horary lineage;
- house significators and the Moon as co-significator;
- applying and separating aspects;
- reception, translation, collection, prohibition, refranation, and frustration;
- considerations before judgment;
- antiscia, solar conditions, and moieties of orbs.

Primary historical line: Dorotheus, Sahl, Masha'allah, Bonatti, and Lilly.

**Status:** planned.

## 4. Elections

**Question:** Which available moment best supports a defined action?

Primary apparatus:

- action classified by house, planet, and topic;
- strength of the relevant significator and angle;
- Moon phase, condition, application, and speed;
- benefic and malefic placement;
- action-specific contraindications;
- planetary hours or lunar mansions when required by the selected lineage.

Primary historical line: Dorotheus, Sahl, Hephaistio, and Bonatti.

**Status:** planned.

## 5. Mundane prediction

**Question:** What does a celestial cycle indicate for a place or collective body?

Primary apparatus:

- Aries and seasonal ingresses for a location;
- solar and lunar eclipses;
- Saturn-Jupiter conjunction cycles;
- mundane lots and geographical rulerships;
- comets and other visible phenomena;
- large-scale planetary periods when supported by the selected lineage.

Primary historical line: Ptolemy, Masha'allah, Abu Ma'shar, and later mundane traditions.

**Status:** planned.

## 6. Medical astrology

**Question:** How does a selected traditional medical lineage describe constitution, illness, crisis,
or treatment timing?

Primary apparatus:

- natal constitution or a decumbiture/illness chart;
- Moon condition and critical days;
- humoral qualities of planets, signs, and elements;
- melothesia;
- crisis aspects;
- treatment elections and planetary hours where appropriate.

Primary historical line: Ptolemaic and Galenic foundations, followed by medieval and early-modern
medical astrology.

**Status:** planned. Its output will be framed explicitly as traditional symbolic material.
